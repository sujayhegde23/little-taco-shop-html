Taco Trivia

when did the first taco appear in India

  • March 2010: Taco Bell opened its first outlet in India at Mantri Square Mall in Bengaluru, bringing the first commercially available tacos to the country.

  • The "Desi" Taco Era: Leading up to recent years, street food vendors and home cooks began making localized "Indian tacos."

  • These variations commonly replace corn or flour tortillas with everyday staples like rotis, theplas, or naan, and stuff them with ingredients like paneer tikka.
